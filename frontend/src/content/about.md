House Lights is an arts events tracker covering theatre, comedy, dance, music, opera, and whatever else is on. It pulls listings from venues across Amsterdam and beyond so there's one place to see what's actually happening without juggling a dozen different websites.

- **Browse** — everything coming up, filtered by type, timeframe, or venue. No account needed.
- **Watchlist** — bookmark shows you're interested in or have bought tickets for, and subscribe to your personal calendar via webcal.
- **Friends** — follow other people and see what they're watching. A good way to find things you'd have otherwise missed.

The scrapers run weekly and write directly to a database, so listings stay reasonably fresh. I've hand-picked which venues to track — if you're signed in, you can hide any venues you never want to see in Settings.

If you know of a venue I'm missing, notice a scraper issue, or have any other ideas, I'd love to hear about it.

It costs a little to run, and if you're getting use out of it a coffee is always appreciated.

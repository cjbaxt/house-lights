House Lights is an Amsterdam arts events tracker covering theatre, comedy, dance, music, opera, and whatever else is on. It scrapes listings from 25+ venues so there's one place to see what's actually happening — without juggling a dozen different websites.

- **Browse** — everything coming up, filtered by type, date range, or venue. No account needed.
- **Watchlist** — bookmark shows you want to see or have bought tickets for. Export to your calendar via webcal so nothing falls through the cracks.
- **Feed** — follow people and see what they're watching. A low-key way to discover things you'd have otherwise missed, and keep track of what your friends are going to.

The scrapers run regularly and write directly to a database, so listings stay current. I've hand-picked which venues to track — if you're signed in, you can hide any venues you never want to see in Settings.

Amsterdam is the focus for now, but the architecture is built to expand to other cities.

If you know of a venue I'm missing, notice a scraper issue, or just want to say hello — I'd love to hear from you.
